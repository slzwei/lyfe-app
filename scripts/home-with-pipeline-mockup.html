What this mockup shows →

Your Home tab gains three new mini-sections between the hero stats and the existing content. Each section shows the 2–3 most-urgent candidates. Tapping a row opens that candidate's detail. Tapping "See all →" opens the full pipeline view.

The 3 sections, in priority order

  • Needs you (red) — candidates flagged at-risk by automatic rules: onboarding stuck, paper failed without re-sit, missed course intake, stuck at same stage 14+ days.
  • This week (butter) — candidates with a scheduled event in the next 5 days: interviews, exams, course starts.
  • Ready (sage) — candidates who just cleared a gate and need you to unlock the next stage: BDM scheduling, RNF filing, etc.

What stays the same

  • Candidates tab — unchanged. Still the search + filter list you have today.
  • Home's existing content — Recent activity, Upcoming events, Roadmap card, Lead pipeline — all live below the new sections.
  • No manager-logging required — urgency signals come from database state the system already tracks (stage transitions, paper attempts, scheduled events).

Tap behaviour

  • Tap a candidate row → their detail page (peek cards view).
  • Tap "See all →" on any section → full pipeline view (the other mockup).
